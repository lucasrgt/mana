The municipal dashboard (the `dashboard` action of
`Hostpoint.Municipalities.VehicleWarning`) counts test accounts and store
reviewers among the travelers in its audience and its monthly cohorts. That
inflates the numbers and can lift a city past the privacy threshold under
which the audience is suppressed (fewer than 10 people). The project's rule
is that every count of people leaves those accounts out. Make the dashboard
follow it.
