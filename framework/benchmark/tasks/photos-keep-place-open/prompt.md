In the travelers app on a phone (the web build in a phone's browser above
all), swiping through a support point's photos, or tapping one, sometimes
throws the traveler back to the map: the touch reaches the map under the
open place, and a tap on the map closes the place.

While a place is open over the map on a phone, the map must take no touch.
Once the place closes, the same map takes touches again, without being
built anew (rebuilding it loses its camera and tiles).
